## 3. Explicit System Boundaries & Documented Limitations

To ensure safe municipal deployment, operators must observe the following documented system boundaries:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Advisory Scope & Physical Actuator Air-Gap**:
> AQUA CREW is designed exclusively as an **advisory and virtual simulation platform**. It generates engineering recommendations, predictive maintenance schedules, and simulation forecasts. It **CANNOT and MUST NOT** be connected directly to real-world municipal SCADA/PLC actuators, pumps, or main valves without certified human engineering oversight and validation.

> [!NOTE]
> **Evidence Provenance Transparency**:
> All insights generated by the platform display their 6-tier evidence classification (`MEASURED`, `SIMULATED`, `DERIVED`, `PREDICTED`, `OPERATOR_ENTERED`, `UNKNOWN`). Decisions impacting public water distribution must give precedence to physical `MEASURED` sensor telemetry over uncalibrated `PREDICTED` machine learning estimates.

> [!TIP]
> **Database Scaling Beyond 10,000 Pipeline Segments**:
> For city-scale networks exceeding 10,000 pipeline segments, deployment of PostgreSQL with PostGIS read replicas and Redis caching for spatial tile queries is recommended.
