# AQUA CREW — TASK 16 SAFETY AND LIMITATIONS
## Virtual Simulation Boundaries, Zero Physical Actuation Guarantee & Safety Guardrails

---

## 1. Zero Physical Actuation Guarantee

> [!CAUTION]
> **ABSOLUTE ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARY: ZERO PHYSICAL ACTUATION**
> AQUA CREW is strictly a **software-first autonomous decision-support, simulation, and inspection planning platform**. Under no circumstances does any module, service, endpoint, or background task actuate physical valves, start physical pumps, operate mechanical excavation equipment, or send control commands to physical programmable logic controllers (PLCs) or SCADA actuators.
>
> All "dispatches", "schedules", "methods", "materials", and "verifications" produced by Task 16 are **virtual simulations** designed for municipal engineering review, resource staging, contractor bidding, and workforce planning.

### Architectural Actuation Safeguards
1. **Isolation from SCADA Output Coils**: API endpoints and background workers possess no hardware drivers, serial interfaces, Modbus write privileges, or OPC-UA control clients.
2. **Read-Only Telemetry Ingestion**: All incoming sensor telemetry (pressure, acoustic decibels, flow) is consumed strictly via read-only message brokers and telemetry caches.
3. **Synthetic Tagging**: All database records and schemas enforce explicit boolean flags:
   - `is_simulated = True` (Default and immutable across all repair models).

## 2. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Engineering Approval

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NO AUTOMATED PHYSICAL REPAIR DISPATCH WITHOUT HUMAN APPROVAL**
> While the Repair Intelligence Service automatically calculates damage assessments, selects repair methods, drafts bills of materials, and runs hydraulic digital twin simulations, **no repair plan can proceed from `ENGINEERING_REVIEW_PENDING` to `APPROVED` or `SCHEDULED` without explicit human sign-off by a certified Professional Engineer (PE)**.

### Enforced Approval Governance
- **Role Enforcement**: Only users with authenticated `ENGINEER` or `ADMINISTRATOR` JWT tokens can call `/plans/{id}/approve` or `/plans/{id}/reject`.
- **Operator Denial**: Attempts by `OPERATOR` or `VIEWER` roles to approve plans are blocked at the API gateway with an immediate `HTTP 403 Forbidden` response.
- **Documented Rationale**: Approvals and rejections require mandatory engineer notes, which are permanently committed to the immutable audit ledger.

## 4. Evidence Completeness Threshold & Fallback Policy

To eliminate catastrophic errors stemming from speculative or hallucinated defects, the system enforces a strict evidence completeness threshold ($C_{\text{evid}}$):

$$C_{\text{evid}} < 50.0\% \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{MANDATORY FALLBACK TO } \texttt{FURTHER\_INSPECTION\_REQUIRED}$$

### Safe Fallback Workflow
1. When critical metadata (e.g. pipe material, wall thickness, verified acoustic leak band) is missing or contradictory, the Repair Method Engine immediately overrides all invasive excavation methods (`CLAMP_REPAIR`, `PIPE_SECTION_REPLACEMENT`).
2. The engine issues a primary recommendation of `FURTHER_INSPECTION_REQUIRED`.
3. It generates a dispatch work order for a non-destructive Task 15 autonomous crawler equipped with ultrasonic wall-thickness gauges and high-resolution optical cameras.
4. Excavation and physical interventions are prohibited until secondary inspection reconciles the missing diagnostic parameters.

## 5. Cryptographic SHA-256 Tamper-Evidence

Post-repair verification certificates issued by `RepairVerificationEngine` are sealed using cryptographic SHA-256 hashing over normalized operational parameters:

$$\mathcal{H} = \text{SHA-256}\left(V_{\text{id}} \;\|\; P_{\text{plan\_id}} \;\|\; S_{\text{segment\_id}} \;\|\; P_{\text{post\_bar}} \;\|\; R_{\text{reduction\_pct}} \;\|\; A_{\text{acoustic}} \;\|\; U_{\text{uncertainty}} \;\|\; U_{\text{engineer\_id}}\right)$$

### Tamper-Evidence Guarantees
- Any unauthorized post-facto modification of measured pressure, water loss reduction, acoustic readings, or inspecting engineer identity produces an immediate hash mismatch.
- Prevents falsification of municipal quality assurance certificates or regulatory water conservation compliance records.
